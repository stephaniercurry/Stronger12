# Stronger / 12

A private, no-login, subscription-free 12-week strength and nutrition tracker.

**Open the app:** https://stephaniercurry.github.io/Stronger12/

## What it does
- Twelve-week progressive strength plan: home or gym workouts on Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday.
- Yoga on Monday and Wednesday; weekends are unscheduled.
- Track sets, repetitions, loads, protein (130 g goal), and fiber (30 g goal).
- Exercise instructions, demonstration video-search links, and progress charts.
- Saves your entries locally in your browser; no account or paid service required.

## iPhone installation
Open the published app in **Safari**, then choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

## Keep your data safe
Use the **Export backup** feature in Settings to save a copy in iCloud Drive. Data isn't automatically cloud-synced, and clearing browser storage may erase your entries.

Source files: `index.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, and icon PNGs. The app is static and hosted with GitHub Pages from the `main` branch root.
